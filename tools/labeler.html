vioreg labeler

Label windows blind

  1. Pick the labels/ folder of the repo (it must contain windows_ui.csv and sheets/). Only those are read; windows.csv is never opened.
  2. Choose the pass, then label. Keys 1-6 label, u undoes, s skips, n edits notes (Esc leaves), h shows the codebook, d downloads.
  3. Progress autosaves in this browser. Download the CSV often and when done.
